# monosyllabic.
September 09, 2022
One of my favorite aspects to marriage is my monosyllabic conversations with my wife Maybelline. 

The most common one is:

Often: Food?
Maybe: Food?
Often: Food!
Maybelline: Food!

Roughly translated, this means:

Often: My dear, I require sustenance. Do you require nutrients to operate as well?
Maybe: Yes, my body has hankering pangs associated with lack of nutriments.  Shall we endeaver to fill this lack posthaste?
Often: Indeed!
Maybe: Righty ho!

And off we go, rummaging our fridge for some comestibles with the fervor of an American pigmy shrew.

I love my wife.
